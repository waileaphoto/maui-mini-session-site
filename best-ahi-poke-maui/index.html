Where to Find the Best Ahi Poke in Maui

Fresh ahi, simple seasoning and plenty of local flavor. Here are seven places to start when you’re looking for great poke on Maui.

Maui is a paradise not just for its stunning beaches and lush landscapes but also for its vibrant and flavorful cuisine. One of the island’s most iconic dishes is ahi poke, a local favorite featuring chunks of raw, marinated ahi tuna. Whether you’re visiting for the first time or call Maui home, discovering the best spots to enjoy this delicacy is a must. Here’s a guide to where you can find some of the best ahi poke on the island.

1. Paia Fish Market

Paia & Kihei

Paia Fish Market is beloved by both locals and visitors for fresh seafood in a casual setting. It’s a convenient stop when you’re exploring Paia or South Maui and want a taste of island-style fish without the formality of a resort restaurant.

2. Foodland

Various locations across Maui

Foodland may be a grocery store, but its poke counter has long been a favorite stop for locals and visitors. The selection often includes Hawaiian-style poke with limu, spicy ahi and classic shoyu preparations. It’s also one of the easiest options for grabbing poke to take to the beach.

3. Kihei Caffe

Kihei

For a laid-back Maui meal, Kihei Caffe is a familiar South Maui name. If ahi is on your mind, it’s worth checking the current menu and specials while enjoying the casual Kihei atmosphere.

4. Poke Hale Grindz

Kahului

Poke Hale Grindz is the kind of Kahului stop worth seeking out when you want bold local flavors away from the resort areas. Look for different ahi preparations and ask what the staff recommends that day.

5. South Maui Fish Company

Kihei

South Maui Fish Company is a favorite for people looking for fresh fish in a casual setting. Their poke bowls have become especially popular, making this a great stop for an easy lunch before or after a South Maui beach day.

6. Island Gourmet Markets

Wailea

Located at The Shops at Wailea, Island Gourmet Markets is especially convenient if you’re staying at one of the Wailea resorts. The market offers prepared foods and poke that are easy to pick up for lunch, a beach picnic or a quick meal back at your hotel.

7. Seafood City

Kahului

Seafood City in Kahului is another worthwhile stop for poke lovers. The market setting makes it easy to explore different seafood preparations and pick up ahi poke along with other local and island-style foods.

Insider Tips for Finding Great Ahi Poke

Go early. Popular poke can sell out, and an earlier visit usually gives you the best selection.

Ask what’s fresh. Selection can change, so ask the counter staff what they recommend that day.

Try different styles. Shoyu, limu, sesame and spicy ahi can taste completely different. Trying a few varieties is part of the fun.

A Taste of Maui

Maui’s poke scene ranges from neighborhood markets to fish counters and casual eateries. Whether you want a quick grab-and-go lunch or something to take down to the beach, ahi poke is one of the simplest and most delicious ways to experience local island food.
