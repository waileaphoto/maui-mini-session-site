Meet the Balter family

Maui Mini Session is photographed by Laurence, Jude, Max, and Solomon Balter—the same family behind Wailea Photo.

Our family made Maui home more than a quarter century ago. Since then, we have photographed families, couples, and weddings throughout Wailea, Makena, and South Maui.

Laurence Balter

Laurence is a professional photographer and founder of Wailea Photo. He writes the parent studio’s Maui Atlas, sharing field knowledge developed through years of photographing Maui’s light, weather, and shoreline.

What family-operated means here

Your booking stays with our family. We do not operate as a marketplace and we do not send an unfamiliar third-party photographer in our place. This continuity matters: the style, direction, editing, and expectations you see before booking are connected to the people who create the photographs.

Two ways to work with us

Maui Mini Session is the concise experience: 20 minutes, 25+ photographs, and simple pricing from $299. Wailea Photo is our parent studio for longer family stories, couples, proposals, maternity, weddings, and larger groups.
