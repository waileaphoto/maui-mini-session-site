What is a turquoise-water session?

It is 20 minutes of guided shoreline photography timed for visible tropical water color, including at least 25 high-resolution edited images for $299, for up to five people.

How turquoise water photographs

Sun position, clouds, wind, and ocean surface all influence color. We select timing and angles to emphasize Maui’s blues while keeping skin tones natural. Because conditions are real, no exact shade of water can be guaranteed.

Who this look suits

It works especially well for families and couples who prefer a bright, tropical feeling over golden-hour drama. White, cream, soft coral, and restrained island color pair naturally with blue water.

Can children play near the water?

Yes, when conditions allow. Movement often produces the most relaxed photographs. Safety and the photographer’s direction come first at every shoreline.

Morning, sunset, or turquoise?

Morning emphasizes softness and calm; sunset emphasizes warmth; turquoise emphasizes tropical color. Return to the three session choices to compare photographs and live availability.
